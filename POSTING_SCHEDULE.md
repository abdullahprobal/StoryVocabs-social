### Key Behavioral Insights for Our Audience

**BCS/Bank/Admission aspirants follow a predictable daily rhythm:**

```
5:30 AM  ── Wake up, Fajr prayer, check phone (morning scroll)
6:00 AM  ── Study session 1 (vocabulary, notes)
8:00 AM  ── Breakfast + social media break ← 📍 POST WINDOW
10:00 AM ── Study session 2
12:30 PM ── Lunch + extended social media scroll ← 📍 POST WINDOW
2:00 PM  ── Study session 3 (practice tests)
4:30 PM  ── Tea break + phone check
6:00 PM  ── Study session 4
8:00 PM  ── Dinner + family time
9:00 PM  ── Social media + entertainment ← 📍 POST WINDOW
10:30 PM ── Light study / revision
11:30 PM ── Pre-sleep phone scroll ← 📍 POST WINDOW
12:00 AM ── Sleep
```

**Critical insight:** Students scroll most during **transition periods** — between study sessions, meals, and before sleep. Content posted during deep study hours (10 AM-12 PM, 2-5 PM) gets saved but not immediately engaged with.

## Daily Posting Schedule (4 Posts/Day)

### Standard Schedule (Non-Ramadan)

| Post | Time | Platform | Content Type | Rationale |
|------|------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Post 1** | 8:00 AM | Facebook + Instagram | 3 words, short story | Catches morning wake-and-scroll. Students check phone after Fajr + breakfast. High save rate for study reference. |
| **Post 2** | 1:00 PM | Facebook + Instagram | 5 words, expanded story | Peak lunch break scrolling. Students eat + scroll simultaneously. Highest share rate of the day. |
| **Post 3** | 8:00 PM | Facebook + Instagram + TikTok | 3 words, short story (different genre) | Evening relaxation peak. Students done with study, browsing for entertainment. Best engagement overall. |
| **Post 4** | 10:30 PM | Facebook + Instagram | 5 words, expanded story | Pre-sleep scroll. Students wind down with phone. High save rate for next-day revision. |

### Why This Schedule Works

```
Post 1 (8:00 AM)
├── Audience: Waking up, checking phone after morning routine
├── Mental state: Fresh, receptive to learning
├── Behavior: Quick scroll before starting study
├── Expected action: Save for later study reference
└── Engagement type: Saves > Shares > Comments > Likes

Post 2 (1:00 PM)
├── Audience: Lunch break, eating + scrolling
├── Mental state: Relaxed, looking for quick entertainment
├── Behavior: Extended scroll session (15-20 min)
├── Expected action: Share with study group friends
└── Engagement type: Shares > Comments > Likes > Saves

Post 3 (8:00 PM)
├── Audience: Done with study, relaxing with family/phone
├── Mental state: Tired but engaged, seeking light content
├── Behavior: Casual browsing, open to new content
├── Expected action: Like + Comment + Follow
└── Engagement type: Likes > Comments > Shares > Saves

Post 4 (10:30 PM)
├── Audience: Pre-sleep phone time
├── Mental state: Reflective, preparing for tomorrow
├── Behavior: Slow scroll, saving useful content
├── Expected action: Save for tomorrow's revision
└── Engagement type: Saves > Likes > Comments > Shares
```

## Ramadan Special Schedule

During Ramadan, the entire daily rhythm shifts. Engagement patterns change dramatically:

```
🌙 RAMADAN SCHEDULE (All times BST)

Pre-Iftar (3:00 PM - 6:00 PM)
├── Students fasting, low energy, high phone usage
├── Best for: Light vocabulary, motivational content
├── Post at: 4:00 PM (pre-iftar scroll peak)

Post-Iftar (7:00 PM - 9:00 PM)
├── Breaking fast, family time, moderate phone usage
├── Best for: Story content, engagement posts
├── Post at: 8:30 PM

Late Night (10:00 PM - 2:00 AM)
├── Peak activity! Students awake, studying, scrolling
├── Best for: All content types, especially vocabulary
├── Post at: 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM

Suhoor (3:00 AM - 4:30 AM)
├── Pre-dawn meal, phone checking
├── Best for: Quick vocabulary posts
├── Post at: 3:30 AM
```

### Ramadan 4-Post Schedule

| Post | Time | Rationale |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Post 1** | 4:00 PM | Pre-iftar peak scroll. Students waiting for iftar, maximum phone time. |
| **Post 2** | 8:30 PM | Post-iftar relaxation. Family time ending, phone browsing begins. |
| **Post 3** | 11:00 PM | Late night study session. Students actively studying + scrolling. |
| **Post 4** | 1:00 AM | Peak late-night activity. Highest engagement of Ramadan period. |

```

```

### Pohela Boishakh (April 14)

```
April 13: Post at 8:00 PM (pre-celebration excitement)
April 14: NO POSTS (national holiday, celebrating)
April 15: Resume normal schedule with Boishakh-themed content
```

### Independence Day (March 26) / Victory Day (December 16)

```
Morning: Post patriotic vocabulary content at 8:00 AM
Evening: Post historical story content at 8:00 PM
```

### Friday (Weekly Holiday)

```
Morning (8 AM): Skip or post light content (people sleeping in)
Lunch (1 PM): Post as normal (people awake, browsing)
Evening (8 PM): Best Facebook day — post your strongest content
Night (10:30 PM): Post as normal
```

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### Facebook (Primary Platform — 50M+ BD users)

```
Best posting times: 8:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 8:00 PM
Best days: Wednesday, Thursday, Friday
Content format: Carousel posts (4-6 slides)
Caption length: 150-300 words with Bangla + English
Hashtags: 5-8 relevant hashtags
CTA: "Save করো", "Share করো তোমার study group-এ"
```

**Facebook-specific tips:**
- Facebook algorithm favors posts that get comments in first 30 minutes
- Reply to every comment within 1 hour to boost reach
- Share posts to relevant Facebook groups (BCS preparation, Bank job prep)
- Use Facebook Stories for behind-the-scenes content

### Instagram (Growing — Urban Gen Z/Millennials)

```
Best posting times: 8:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 8:00 PM, 10:30 PM
Best days: Monday-Thursday
Content format: Carousel + Reels
Reels best time: 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Stories best time: 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM
```

**Instagram-specific tips:**
- Reels get 3x more reach than carousel posts
- Post Reels at 8:00 PM for maximum FYP exposure
- Use trending Bangla audio for Reels
- Stories with polls/quizzes get highest engagement
- Post to Explore-optimized hashtags

### TikTok (Exploding — Younger audience)

```
Best posting times: 4:00 PM, 8:00 PM, 10:30 PM
Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
Content format: 30-60 second vocabulary videos
```

**TikTok-specific tips:**
- Post 2-3 hours before peak engagement (algorithm needs time to test)
- Hook must grab attention in first 3 seconds
- Use trending sounds with educational overlay
- Reply to comments immediately to boost engagement

## Engagement Optimization Checklist

### Before Posting

- [ ] Check if today is a holiday/special occasion
- [ ] Verify content matches today's genre schedule
- [ ] Ensure caption has Bangla + English mix
- [ ] Add relevant hashtags (5-8 for Facebook, 10-15 for Instagram)
- [ ] Include clear CTA (Save, Share, Comment, Download)
- [ ] Tag relevant accounts if applicable

### After Posting (First 2 Hours)

- [ ] Reply to every comment within 30 minutes
- [ ] Share post to 2-3 relevant Facebook groups
- [ ] Post to Instagram Stories with "New Post" sticker
- [ ] Engage with similar accounts' posts (algorithm boost)
- [ ] Monitor engagement rate in first hour

### Weekly Review

- [ ] Check Meta Business Suite insights
- [ ] Identify best-performing post of the week
- [ ] Note which time slot got highest engagement
- [ ] Adjust next week's schedule based on data
- [ ] Track follower growth trend
